Everything Nova does on your desktop

Wake word, hands-free

Say “Hello Nova” from across the room. Nova runs silently in the tray — no window until you ask for one.

Memory that updates itself

Tell Nova about your goals and people. When something changes, the old fact is replaced, not duplicated.

Controls your PC

Open apps and accounts, search, adjust volume, read the screen aloud and translate text under the mouse.

Reminders & prayer times

Morning check-ins, focus sessions, reminders and namaz alerts calculated for your city.

Reads your notifications

WhatsApp, Outlook, Teams and unread browser tabs — spoken aloud so you don’t have to look.

Private by default

The desktop app runs on a local model with Ollama. Your memory stays in a file on your computer.

Install Nova on Windows

  1. Download and unzip Nova anywhere, e.g. Documents\Nova.
  2. Right-click install.ps1 → Run with PowerShell. It installs the Python packages and the local AI models.
  3. Say “Hello Nova”. Nova starts with Windows and waits quietly in the tray.

Needs Windows 10/11, Python 3.10–3.13, Ollama and about 6 GB of free disk space.
